**Logic**: 10x more buyers at 10% price = same revenue but FASTER
**Impact**: Lower barrier → more conversions → faster cash

**Impact**: Receive payment in seconds not days

#### 11. Managed Trading Service

#### 12. Trading Bot as a Service (SaaS)
