## What AI Nexus IS (Internal System)

AI Nexus is NOT just a brand name - it's an actual production system:
- **Multi-Brain Orchestration**: Claude, ChatGPT, Copilot coordinated
- **Budget Management**: Daily spending limits per provider ($100 Copilot, $50 each for ChatGPT/Claude/OpenAI)
- **Full Audit Trail**: Every AI operation tracked
- **Financial Ledger**: ROI tracking for self-financing
- **Intelligent Task Routing**: Auto-selects best AI for each task

### Core Files
- `ai_nexus/nexus.py` - Central orchestrator
- `ai_nexus/ledger.py` - Financial tracking
- `ai_nexus/copilot_integration.py` - GitHub Copilot wrapper
