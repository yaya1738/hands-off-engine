- "Need AI expert" with no specifics (tire kickers)

- "Looking for cheap" (value mismatch)

## Green Flags (Prioritize)

- Budget > $1000 or hourly > $50
- Verified payment method
- Previous hires with good reviews
- Clear deliverables defined
