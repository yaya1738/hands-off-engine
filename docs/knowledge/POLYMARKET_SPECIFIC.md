## Capital Efficiency: Reusable Collateral

### How It Works
- Cash backs limit orders as collateral
- BUT cash isn't locked until ORDER FILLS
- Same cash can collateralize MULTIPLE open limit orders
- Only locked when order actually executes

### Example
- Have $100
- Post $100 limit on Market A, B, C, D, E...
- All valid simultaneously
- When one fills → that $100 locked → others at risk if they also fill

### Strategic Implications

**Infinite patience enabled:**
- No pressure to deploy immediately
- Post at "ridiculous" levels and wait
- Fish for horrible fills (horrible for counterparty)
- Let the market come to you

**Conservative capital deployment:**
- Don't need to take current prices
- Same $100 fishing everywhere at once
- No penalty for patience
- Premium for waiting for great entries

**The strategy:**
- Be liquidity provider at extreme levels
- Let desperate/dumb money come to you
- Collect edge from bad fills, not from chasing
- Market make across many markets with small capital

### Risk Management
- If multiple orders fill simultaneously → might not have enough
- Monitor and cancel if getting close
- Order management matters

### Dynamic Order Management = Next Level
- Cancel and reopen instantly, no penalty
- Move orders around ALL of Polymarket in real-time
- Not static limits sitting there - ACTIVE management

**The Matrix:**
- Every market has fill probability per price level
- Your capital = distributed across this entire matrix
- As conditions change → redistribute
- Other participants moving too → dynamic system

**Optimization variables:**
- Fill probability at each level
- Edge if filled at that level
- Time to resolution
- Current flow/activity
- Spread changes
- Competition from other market makers

**Automation advantage:**
- Humans can't track all markets simultaneously
- Bot can monitor entire matrix
- React in milliseconds
- Rebalance continuously
- Calculate EV per order placement in real-time

## Market Participation Impact

### You ARE the Market
- Every order you place changes the book
- Every fill moves price
- Your presence affects others' decisions
- You're not observing - you're PARTICIPATING

### Self-Impact Awareness
**On entry:** Your bid raises best bid, others see it, might front-run

**On exit:** Your sell pressure visible, can move price against yourself

**On limits:** You add depth, others react, pulling orders = signal too

### Feedback Loops
- Your action → market reaction → affects your next action
- Can't assume static conditions
- What you saw BEFORE you act ≠ what exists AFTER you act

### Strategic Implications
- Don't show hand unnecessarily
- Size appropriately to minimize impact
- Consider breaking up large orders
- Edge calculation must include self-impact cost
- The edge you SEE isn't the edge you GET if your order moves market

**The paradox:** Best opportunities in thin markets, but thin markets = YOU move them most

## CRITICAL: Market Orders and Book Depth (Step One)

### The Danger
Your order size vs book depth:
- Want to buy $100
- Best ask only has $20 size
- Market order takes $20 at best, $30 at next level, $50 at worst level
- Blended fill WAY worse than "the price"

### What Just Happened
- Filled at unknown average price
- Ate through multiple levels
- Paid way more than expected
- Moved market against yourself
- Signaled demand to everyone
- **Didn't even realize**

### "The Price" is a LIE
- There is no single price
- There's a BOOK of prices
- Your fill depends on YOUR size vs THEIR sizes
- Best bid/ask = only first layer

### The Fix
1. ALWAYS check book depth BEFORE order
2. Compare your size to available liquidity at each level
3. Know expected fill price (walk the book mentally)
4. Use limit orders to cap worst case
5. Or break up into smaller pieces

**This is step one because everything else is pointless if you don't get this.**
