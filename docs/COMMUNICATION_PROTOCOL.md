# Human communication operating model

The goal is not zero communication. The goal is **zero routine operator work**.

The autonomous system should decide and act within its existing authority,
then communicate concise, decision-relevant information to Yair. Yair can
respond through the same channel, and ordinary responses become durable tasks.

Routine progress is batched. Material decisions and genuine external blockers
are escalated. Human responses never bypass safety, approval, credential, or
execution boundaries.
